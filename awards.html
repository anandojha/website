Awards & Fellowships

Awarded by the Frankfurt Institute for Advanced Studies (FIAS), Frankfurt, Germany, covering conference registration, hotel accommodation, and travel expenses for the Giersch International Conference & SCALE Kick-Off 2026.
2025 – 2026
AI 2030 Global Fellowship
Selected as one of 50 Global Fellows for the prestigious AI 2030 Global Fellowship (2025–2026), a year-long leadership and engagement program focused on advancing Responsible AI. As a Fellow, I collaborate with international leaders across academia, industry, and policy to shape ethical AI frameworks, contribute to initiatives such as the Responsible AI Marketplace and the Global AI Regulation Index, and engage in global dialogues at forums including the G20 TechSprint, IMF/WBG meetings, and the AI 2030 Summit.
2025
Best Poster Award, Computational Cryo-EM Workshop ($500)
Awarded Best Poster Award at the 2025 Computational Cryo-EM Workshop in New York City, sponsored by PRX Life (American Physical Society), with a $500 prize. The poster, titled “cryoWEight: Towards an Automated Python Framework for Iterative Ensemble Cryo-EM-Guided Reweighting for Biomolecular Complexes,” presented a novel open-source pipeline that integrates weighted ensemble simulations with cryo-EM data to accelerate convergence of free energy landscapes and enhance mechanistic interpretation of biomolecular dynamics.

Flatiron Research Fellowship ($270,000)
Awarded the prestigious Flatiron Research Fellowship at the Flatiron Institute, Simons Foundation, a highly selective research fellow position supporting independent and collaborative research in computational science. Fellows are selected based on research excellence, grant writing ability, computational proficiency, and potential for cross-disciplinary impact. Hosted within one of the Institute’s five centers, the fellowship offers full financial support, access to world-class computing infrastructure, and engagement in a vibrant research community that advances theory, modeling, simulation, and data-driven discovery.
